# Problem Space — Cerebrum-Client

> The problem this project solves, who it's for, and why it matters.

## Problem statement

Self-directed learners have no reliable way to know what they actually understand
versus what they only think they understand. Note-taking apps store knowledge but
don't interrogate it. Trauma, ADHD, or just time can silently degrade recall
without the learner realising it.

## Users

- Self-directed learners studying outside formal institutions
- People with memory/recall challenges (PTSD, ADHD, etc.)
- Anyone who wants honest assessment of their knowledge

## Goals

- [ ] Honest, grounded feedback on what you know vs. what you think you know
- [ ] Fully offline-first — works without internet, syncs when available
- [ ] All AI feedback grounded in user's own documents (RAG, no hallucination)
- [ ] Multiple study modes: analysis, flashcards, MCQs, short/long questions
- [ ] Mastery tracking over time with spaced repetition (SM-2)

## Non-goals

- Not a replacement for formal education or human teachers
- Not a general-purpose note-taking app
- Not a cloud service — everything runs locally

## Success metrics

- User can identify gaps before an exam/deadline
- Core quiz/flashcard flows work fully offline
- Analysis feedback cites user-provided documents (no hallucination)
